Verified this pass. Damage-bucket structure was cross-referenced against multiple damage guides: Critical Strike Damage and Vulnerable Damage bonuses are additive into the same bucket as your other +% stats, while the base Critical (×1.5) and Vulnerable (×1.2) effects are separate fixed multipliers. Strength is its own multiplicative bucket. The engine implements exactly this.

Fixed this pass. A Unique/Mythic item's power used to live in a separate Multipliers row with no connection to whether the item was actually equipped — equipping Ramaladni's Magnum Opus or Ring of Starless Skies on a gear slot did nothing unless you separately found and enabled a same-named row elsewhere, and the shipped sample build itself demonstrated this (both were "equipped" but contributed zero damage). Each known Unique/Mythic power now lives directly on its gear slot's editor — selecting it there is what applies it, and its live value is shown on the paperdoll slot itself. A second bug was fixed alongside it: loading the sample build handed out live references to its arrays/objects, so editing a loaded sample would silently corrupt the built-in sample for the rest of the session.

Verified this pass. Ramaladni's Magnum Opus was checked against its live tooltip (fextralife, 2026-08-22): "Skills using this weapon deal [0.35–0.50]% increased damage per point of Fury you have, but you lose 10 Fury every second." The engine's maxFury × %perFury formula matches; the two inputs now default to the midpoint of the confirmed roll range and are clamped to it. The Fury-drain clause isn't modeled as a rotational mechanic — "Max Fury" is still a static stand-in for sustained Fury, same simplification as before, just now on a confirmed base number. Ring of Starless Skies was also checked (same source, same date): "Spending your Primary Resource ... increases your damage by 10% for 3 seconds, up to 50%" — matches the existing 10%/stack, 0–5 stacks, 50%-cap formula exactly, no change needed. Gohr's Devastating Grips ("Whirlwind deals [75–90%] increased damage") and Tuskhelm of Joritz the Mighty ("...granting [40–50%] increased damage...") were previously hardcoded flat guesses (82% and 42%) — both are actually roll ranges, so they're now user-adjustable within the confirmed range instead of fixed, same pattern as Ramaladni's. Berserking's own tooltip ("Increases your damage by 25% and your movement speed by 30%") also matches the existing 25% exactly. The three Aspects that previously had no trustworthy value at all are now confirmed and default to their roll midpoint: Aspect of Channeling [50–70]% while channeling a skill (Whirlwind qualifies), Aspect of Anger Management [15.0–20.0]% while above 50 Fury, and Crushing Aspect (listed here as "Aspect of Crushing") [45–65]% while Fortified. None of these three model the extra +50%/+100% potency an Aspect gets on an Amulet or Two-Handed Weapon slot — adjust the number by hand if that's where you have it socketed.

Added, deliberately shallow. Three Paragon Glyph rows (Wrath, Exploit, Marshal) add their flat "Legendary Bonus" tag — each +10%, all confirmed via Game8 (checked 2026-08-22), unlocking at Glyph rank 46 and unchanged through rank 100, the highest rank anything publishes despite the real cap being 150. Treated as the practical rank-150 value rather than chasing an unpublished curve. This is intentionally not a full Paragon simulator: the radius/attribute-scaling part of these same Glyphs, Twister's separate Dust Devil damage, the five Legendary Nodes, and Challenger (no reliable Barbarian numbers found) are all left out — that level of fidelity would need the exact board pathing and turn this into a different tool.

Flagged this pass, not fixed with a guess. Wrath of the Berserker previously carried a flat +30% damage value, but its live tooltip ("Explode into rage, Knocking Back surrounding enemies while gaining Berserking and Unstoppable for 10 seconds") shows no separate damage bonus of its own — it just grants the Berserking status, which is already its own toggle above. Toggling both on, as the sample build did, may have been double-counting one real bonus as two. Rather than invent a replacement number, it's been zeroed and marked — confirm from your own character sheet whether WotB grants anything beyond Berserking before re-enabling it.

Resolved this pass, with history kept visible. The Strength coefficient looked contested because most sources report a generic, non-class-specific number. Maxroll's live Stats for Beginners page breaks it out per class: Barbarian specifically gets +0.11% Skill Damage per point of Strength (9.09 Strength per 1%) — different from Druid/Paladin/Sorcerer/Necromancer/Spiritborn, which get +0.125% (8 per 1%). That 0.125%/8 figure is almost certainly what earlier passes (and a naive Google summary) picked up and wrongly generalized to Barbarian. The older Fextralife stat page (last touched 2024) and Game8 both round to 10 Strength = 1% (+0.1%/point) — close to Maxroll's number but not exact, and older. Default is now Maxroll's Barbarian-specific 9.09, , still a plain input if your own sheet disagrees.

Not verified — treat with suspicion. Rows marked have no trustworthy number at all and default to 0.

Why not more. This build ran without network access to Diablo databases and build-planner sites, so per-item affix values, Paragon node values, and glyph scaling could not be pulled. Anything item-specific has to come off your own character sheet. Nothing here is invented to fill a gap — where a number is unknown it is 0 and flagged.

hit = weapon × skill% × strength × Π(multipliers) × (1 + additive% [+ crit] [+ vuln]) × 1.5crit × 1.2vuln
DPS is the average across all four crit/vulnerable outcomes × attack speed. Whirlwind attack speed uses Season 14 frame breakpoints, so it steps rather than scaling smoothly.
