Verified this pass. Damage-bucket structure was cross-referenced against multiple damage guides: Critical Strike Damage and Vulnerable Damage bonuses are additive into the same bucket as your other +% stats, while the base Critical (×1.5) and Vulnerable (×1.2) effects are separate fixed multipliers. Strength is its own multiplicative bucket. The engine implements exactly this.

Fixed this pass. A Unique/Mythic item's power used to live in a separate Multipliers row with no connection to whether the item was actually equipped — equipping Ramaladni's Magnum Opus or Ring of Starless Skies on a gear slot did nothing unless you separately found and enabled a same-named row elsewhere, and the shipped sample build itself demonstrated this (both were "equipped" but contributed zero damage). Each known Unique/Mythic power now lives directly on its gear slot's editor — selecting it there is what applies it, and its live value is shown on the paperdoll slot itself. A second bug was fixed alongside it: loading the sample build handed out live references to its arrays/objects, so editing a loaded sample would silently corrupt the built-in sample for the rest of the session.

Not verified — treat with suspicion. The Strength coefficient is genuinely contested: sources report both 10 Strength = 1% and 10 Strength = 0.8% damage. It's exposed as an input in Setup rather than hardcoded. The four gear-bound Unique/Mythic values (Ramaladni's Magnum Opus, Ring of Starless Skies, Gohr's, Tuskhelm) and the Berserking/Wrath of the Berserker multipliers carry over from this tool's earlier versions and were not re-verified against live tooltips this pass — check each against your own game. Rows marked have no trustworthy number at all and default to 0.

Why not more. This build ran without network access to Diablo databases and build-planner sites, so per-item affix values, Paragon node values, and glyph scaling could not be pulled. Anything item-specific has to come off your own character sheet. Nothing here is invented to fill a gap — where a number is unknown it is 0 and flagged.

hit = weapon × skill% × strength × Π(multipliers) × (1 + additive% [+ crit] [+ vuln]) × 1.5crit × 1.2vuln
DPS is the average across all four crit/vulnerable outcomes × attack speed. Whirlwind attack speed uses Season 14 frame breakpoints, so it steps rather than scaling smoothly.
