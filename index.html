Attack Speed uses real frame breakpoints: Whirlwind's animation is checked every few engine frames, not continuously — so APS jumps in steps rather than scaling smoothly with AS%. This tool uses the published Season 14 Whirlwind breakpoint table (Maxroll Barbarian Compendium) for your chosen weapon speed category, split into Cap 1 and Cap 2 Attack Speed sources (each individually capped at 100%). Frame data is an engine-level property of the skill's animation — Blizzard doesn't reshuffle it every season by default, but skill reworks or balance patches can shift it, so it's worth a quick sanity check against your in-game APS if a new season has just landed. This table is Whirlwind-specific; other Barbarian skills have their own separate breakpoint tables and are not covered by this tool.
