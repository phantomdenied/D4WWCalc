This tool estimates your Whirlwind Barbarian's damage per second (DPS) from the numbers on your character screen, skill tooltip, and gear. Where do I find these numbers in-game?
Weapon Min/Max Damage and its Attack Speed % are on your weapon's tooltip. Strength is on your Character Screen (default key C), under Attributes. Skill % is on Whirlwind's own tooltip in your skill bar. Crit Chance, Crit Damage %, and Vulnerable Damage % are on the Character Screen under "Offense".
Additive vs. Multiplicative — what's the difference?
Additive stats (Section 3) add together first: +50% and +30% become one +80% bonus. Multiplicative stats (Section 4, written "x%") each apply as their own separate factor: two +20% multipliers become ×1.2 × ×1.2 = ×1.44, not ×1.40. The game keeps these two buckets separate, so this tool does too — it's the single biggest thing to get right.
Crit & Vulnerable, in plain terms
Critical Strike is a % chance for a hit to deal bonus damage (a fixed ×1.5 here). Vulnerable is a debuff you put on enemies that makes every hit against them deal bonus damage (a fixed ×1.2). "Vulnerable Uptime" is roughly how much of a fight the enemy is Vulnerable for — 100% if you can keep it up constantly.
Don't have every number handy yet?
That's fine — every field starts pre-filled with a reasonable value for a mid-to-late-game Whirlwind build. Change only what you know for now and refine the rest later; nothing needs to be perfect to get a useful estimate.

Once you're comfortable, Advanced Mode lets you enter every individual affix off every equipped item instead of lump-sum totals — handy for comparing two specific pieces of gear, but not required to get started. Most players should stay in Basic Mode.

New to this tool? Stay in Basic Mode — it covers everything most builds need. Advanced Mode is for entering exact per-item affixes, useful once you're comparing specific gear upgrades.

Attack Speed uses real frame breakpoints: Whirlwind's animation is checked every few engine frames, not continuously — so APS jumps in steps rather than scaling smoothly with AS%. This tool uses the published Season 14 Whirlwind breakpoint table (Maxroll Barbarian Compendium) for your chosen weapon speed category, split into Cap 1 and Cap 2 Attack Speed sources (each individually capped at 100%). Frame data is an engine-level property of the skill's animation — Blizzard doesn't reshuffle it every season by default, but skill reworks or balance patches can shift it, so it's worth a quick sanity check against your in-game APS if a new season has just landed. This table is Whirlwind-specific; other Barbarian skills have their own separate breakpoint tables and are not covered by this tool.
